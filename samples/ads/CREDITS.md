# Sample advert photographs — sources and licences

The pictures in this folder are the demo library's sample adverts. Each one is
an invented advert for an invented brand (Northbank Roasters, Northbank Cold
Brew, Brightbrew). The headline, supporting line, call to action and logo are
drawn by `scripts/make-sample-ads.mjs`; nothing in them is a real company, a
real mark, a real product or a real contact detail.

The photograph under each advert is openly licensed stock from Wikimedia
Commons, listed below with its author, its licence and the page it came from.
Every one is CC0 or public domain, so the composite above may be re-used on
the same terms. No photograph here was bought, generated or taken from a paid
stock library.

Re-draw them with `node scripts/make-sample-ads.mjs`, which fetches these
photographs again from the recorded URLs.
